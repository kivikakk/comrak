---
title: Subscript
---

```````````````````````````````` example -e subscript
H~2~O
.
<p>H<sub>2</sub>O</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example -e subscript
f~i~ = f~i - 2~ + f~i - 1~.
.
<p>f<sub>i</sub> = f<sub>i - 2</sub> + f<sub>i - 1</sub>.</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example -e subscript -e strikethrough
~~H~2~O~~
.
<p><del>H<sub>2</sub>O</del></p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example -e subscript
~~H~2~O~~
.
<p>~~H<sub>2</sub>O~~</p>
````````````````````````````````
